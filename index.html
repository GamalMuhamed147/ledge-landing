FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out and our team will get back to you within a day.

Most teams import their properties and units and are sending their first renewal reminder within a day. Our onboarding specialists can help migrate data from spreadsheets or another platform at no extra cost on Growth and Enterprise plans.

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your billing settings. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated for the current cycle; downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period.

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial and no credit card is required to begin. You'll only be asked for billing details if you decide to continue after the trial ends.

Starter and Growth are priced per portfolio, not per unit, so growing your portfolio within a plan's limits never increases your bill. Once you're managing several hundred units or need custom SLAs, our Enterprise plan is tailored to your team's size and structure.

Growth and Enterprise plans include shared team chat, task assignment, and role-based permissions, so property managers, accountants, and maintenance staff each see exactly what's relevant to them.

You can export every property, lease, and payment record at any time from your account settings. If you cancel, your data remains available for export for 30 days before it's permanently deleted.
